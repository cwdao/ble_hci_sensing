# 一、三个现有贡献是否准确？

你们当前的三个贡献是：

1. 首次全面建模 BLE CS 呼吸感知，识别三个物理约束；
2. 提出统一管线解决三个约束；
3. 在金属板和真人场景上验证。

方向基本正确，但需要调整措辞和重心。

## C1：有意义，但“物理约束”要重新定义

当前 C1 是最有潜力的贡献。但第三个约束目前写成：

> 顺序 tone 扫描引入超越 $\pm1$ 的连续相位偏移。

这个因果关系并没有被现有实验充分证明。你们自己的时序计算表明，完整扫描在呼吸频率上只造成几度相位差，因此观察到的连续 tone 间相位更可能来自：

- 频率选择性多径；
- 不同 tone 的复信道灵敏度方向；
- 射频链路和校准残差；
- 低采样率下的滤波、插值及窗口效应；
- 顺序采样的少量附加影响。

因此建议将三个“约束”改为三个**measurement characteristics**：

1. **双向 PCT 产生两个端侧幅值观测及一个组合相位观测**，三者相关但噪声结构与复平面投影不同；
2. **稀疏且可能不均匀的事件采样**使短窗时域估计更敏感，而谱域对 BPM 更稳健；
3. **频率选择性多径和实现非理想性导致 tone 间及模态间存在连续、场景相关的相位关系**，不能仅用 $\pm1$ 符号描述。

这样 C1 是准确且有意义的。

“首次”可以保留，但必须限定为：

> To the best of our knowledge, this is the first systematic characterization of BLE Channel Sounding for contactless respiration sensing.

不要写“first BLE CS sensing work”，因为你们自己已经有一篇 BLE CS device-free sensing 的早期演示工作；当前论文应明确区别为从“可行性演示”推进到“系统建模、算法设计和人体呼吸验证”。已有公开工作将 BLE 6.0 CS 用于 device-free sensing 的早期探索描述为首个 CS sensing demonstration，因此本篇最安全的 first claim 是“first systematic characterization for respiration sensing”。([doi.org](https://doi.org/10.1145/3714394.3754369?utm_source=openai))

------

## C2：有意义，但不要宣称“统一解决全部三个物理约束”

当前 C2 的问题不是方法不够好，而是叙述过满：

> unified pipeline addressing all three constraints.

其中：

- BPM 谱分支有很强的证据；
- tone-level quality voting 有明显增益；
- 波形两级 Hilbert 对齐也有实验依据；
- 但三模态等权并非所有数据上都最好；
- Phase 的角色仍然场景依赖；
- “unlocking effect”的统计可靠性还需加强；
- 顺序扫描也尚未被证明是连续相位偏移的主因。

更安全的 C2 应强调**任务分解**：

> 一个 measurement-aware、task-oriented 的双输出管线：谱域分支负责 BPM，复平面相干分支负责波形。

这实际上是你们很有价值的设计思想：

- 不强迫同一个融合结果同时服务 BPM 和波形；
- BPM 只需要频率，不必承担脆弱的时域相干估计；
- 波形需要保留形态，因此需要连续相位对齐；
- 两条分支共享 tone 质量评估，但使用不同的融合域。

这比“又提出一种加权方法”更有论文价值。

------

## C3：现在偏弱，应从“做了实验”升级为“获得可推广的设计结论”

“在金属板和真人场景验证”本身通常不够构成独立贡献，因为任何系统论文都应该验证。

建议把 C3 改成：

> 通过可控机械目标和人体呼吸两个域的系统实验，量化 BLE CS 中 tone diversity、modal diversity、谱域融合和相干融合各自的贡献，并揭示模态融合具有明显场景依赖性。

你们真正有价值的实验发现包括：

- tone/channel 融合是最稳定、最大的增益来源；
- 谱域融合更适合 BPM；
- 连续相位对齐对波形比 $\pm1$ 符号修正更有效；
- Remote 和 Local 在总体上物理对等；
- Phase 在真人和机械目标上的表现显著不同；
- 模态融合并非无条件有益；
- 从 WiFi 迁移来的 PCA、MRC、PCA-VMD 并不能完整适应 BLE CS。

这已经不只是“validation”，而是对后续 BLE CS sensing 研究有指导意义的**measurement study**。

## 英文版本

> **This paper makes the following contributions:**
>
> 1. **BLE CS respiration observation model and empirical characterization.** We formulate the bidirectional PCT measurements as two endpoint amplitude observables and one composite-phase observable, and characterize their complex-plane sensitivities, inter-tone relationships, and scene-dependent inter-modal phase offsets. Our analysis reveals why BLE CS respiration sensing cannot be treated as a direct low-rate migration of WiFi CSI sensing.
> 2. **A task-oriented dual-branch sensing pipeline.** We develop BreatheCS, which shares a quality-aware tone fusion front-end but separates respiratory-rate estimation from waveform recovery. The results quantify the dominant benefit of tone diversity, demonstrate the different fusion requirements of BPM and waveform recovery, and reveal that modal diversity—especially composite phase—is beneficial only under scene- and quality-dependent conditions.

这个版本不依赖尚未验证的“Phase 无零陷”，也不依赖“顺序扫描一定导致连续相位”。

# 三、整篇论文最合适的中心论点

Introduction、Abstract、Related Work 和 Conclusion 应围绕同一句话展开：

> **BLE CS is not simply WiFi CSI sampled at a lower rate; its bidirectional measurement structure, sparse event sampling, and frequency-dependent phase relationships require task-specific fusion for respiratory rate and waveform sensing.**

中文：

> **BLE CS 并不是低采样率版本的 WiFi CSI：其双向 PCT 观测结构、稀疏事件采样及频率相关的连续相位关系，使呼吸率估计和波形恢复需要不同的融合原则。**

# 四、Introduction 应该怎么写？

建议写成六段，而不是一开始就堆 BLE CS 细节。

## 第 1 段：问题与应用价值

回答：

- 为什么非接触呼吸感知重要？
- 为什么现有穿戴设备不总是理想？
- 为什么无线设备适合长期、隐私友好的被动感知？

这一段不要写算法，不要写 72 tone。

### 示例

> Respiration is a fundamental physiological indicator for sleep monitoring, chronic disease management, and early detection of respiratory abnormalities. Conventional monitoring solutions typically require wearable belts, electrodes, or dedicated radar hardware, which can limit comfort and long-term deployment. Device-free sensing using commodity wireless signals offers an attractive alternative because it can capture respiration-induced channel variations without requiring the user to wear a sensor.

------

## 第 2 段：为什么 BLE Channel Sounding 值得研究？

回答：

- BLE 广泛部署；
- Bluetooth Core 6.0 引入 Channel Sounding；
- CS 本来为高精度安全测距设计，但同时暴露多频点的相位相关测量；
- 因而具有感知潜力。

Bluetooth Channel Sounding 是 Bluetooth Core 6.0 引入的标准功能，包含 PBR 和 RTT，并在连接的 Initiator–Reflector 设备间运行。([bluetooth.com](https://www.bluetooth.com/core-specification-6-feature-overview/?utm_source=openai))

但不要再写：

> every smartphone already provides BLE CS.

目前这仍然过强。更安全的是：

> BLE is widely deployed, and CS is becoming available in a new generation of BLE 6.0-capable chipsets.

### 示例

> Bluetooth Channel Sounding, introduced in Bluetooth Core 6.0 for secure fine ranging, exposes bidirectional phase-related measurements over multiple RF channels. This creates a new opportunity for device-free sensing on energy-efficient BLE platforms. However, CS was designed for ranging rather than physiological sensing, and its measurement structure differs substantially from the WiFi CSI commonly used in prior respiration systems.

------

## 第 3 段：为什么 WiFi 方法不能直接迁移？

这是 Introduction 最关键的一段。建议用三个 difference，而不是三个过强的“物理约束”。

### Difference 1：观测结构不同

WiFi 通常提供单向复 CSI 或 antenna/subcarrier ratios；BLE CS 提供双向 PCT，经组合后得到两个端侧幅值和一个复合相位。

### Difference 2：采样条件不同

BLE CS 事件率只有约 2–4 Hz；20 秒窗口中只有约 2–7 个呼吸周期，因此短窗时域估计对噪声、event jitter 和非平稳运动更敏感。

### Difference 3：融合关系不同

tone 和模态之间不是固定同相/反相，而是存在连续、场景相关的相位偏差。不能直接把 WiFi 的 PCA sign 或简单 MRC 原样套用。

### 示例

> Directly migrating WiFi respiration pipelines to BLE CS is problematic for three reasons. First, a CS procedure produces two endpoint PCT measurements whose amplitudes remain separately observable, while their phases are combined to suppress reciprocal oscillator offsets. Second, the practical CS event rate is sparse relative to typical WiFi CSI streams, making short-window temporal alignment sensitive to timing irregularity and noise. Third, frequency-selective multipath and implementation nonidealities produce continuous inter-tone and inter-modal phase offsets, whereas many existing fusion methods assume only a binary in-phase/anti-phase relationship.

------

## 第 4 段：你们的关键实验洞察

不要马上介绍完整算法，先告诉读者“我们观察到了什么”。

建议提出三条 insight：

1. tone diversity 是主要增益来源；
2. BPM 和 waveform 需要不同融合域；
3. modal diversity 有条件性，不能硬编码固定偏好。

### 示例

> Our measurements lead to three design insights. First, reliable tone aggregation provides the largest and most consistent gain because individual CS tones exhibit strongly varying respiration sensitivity. Second, respiratory-rate estimation and waveform recovery have different requirements: magnitude spectra are robust to residual temporal offsets, whereas waveform reconstruction requires coherent phase alignment. Third, the three observables should not be assumed equally reliable in every scene; in particular, the composite phase behaves differently between controlled mechanical motion and human respiration.

这一段能自然容纳最新 Phase 研究，而不必在当前版本强行提出 Phase rescue 算法。

------

## 第 5 段：方法概述

用一段介绍 BreatheCS：

- shared front-end；
- spectral BPM branch；
- coherent waveform branch；
- 不要展开所有公式。

### 示例

> Guided by these observations, we design BreatheCS, a measurement-aware dual-branch pipeline. A shared front-end evaluates the respiratory relevance and spectral reliability of each tone. The BPM branch fuses quality-weighted spectra and estimates the dominant respiratory frequency without requiring temporal waveform alignment. In parallel, the waveform branch represents each band-limited signal as an analytic signal and performs hierarchical complex-domain alignment, first across tones and then across usable observables, to reconstruct a coherent respiratory waveform.

如果最终 Phase adaptive gate 做成功，可以加入一句：

> A quality/consensus mechanism determines whether composite phase contributes useful modal diversity.

如果没有成功，就不要在 Introduction 承诺。

# 五、Abstract 应该怎么写？

Abstract 最好保持六个功能句：

1. 背景；
2. 空白；
3. 建模发现；
4. 方法；
5. 实验；
6. 结论和意义。

目前摘要的问题是：

- 信息过密；
- 过早强调“unlocking interaction”；
- 把顺序扫描写成已确认因果；
- “first systematic analysis”与“all three constraints”都过强；
- 数值精度不统一；
- 没有突出 tone diversity 是最大收益来源；
- 没有说明 0.41 BPM 是联合管线结果，而非所有内部变体中的最低点。

## 推荐英文摘要

> **Abstract—** Bluetooth Channel Sounding (CS) exposes multi-frequency bidirectional measurements that create a new opportunity for contactless respiration sensing on energy-efficient BLE devices. However, CS differs fundamentally from WiFi CSI: it provides two endpoint amplitude observations and a composite-phase observation, operates at a sparse practical event rate, and exhibits frequency- and scene-dependent phase relationships that make conventional CSI fusion unreliable.
>
> We present BreatheCS, a systematic modeling and sensing framework for respiratory-rate estimation and waveform recovery with BLE CS. We first formulate the respiration-induced perturbations of the bidirectional PCT measurements and characterize the relationships across tones and observation modalities. The analysis shows that individual tones have highly heterogeneous sensing quality, residual phase offsets are generally continuous rather than binary, and respiratory-rate estimation and waveform reconstruction require different fusion principles. Based on these findings, BreatheCS uses a shared quality-aware tone-fusion front-end, a spectral branch for timing-robust BPM estimation, and a hierarchical complex-domain alignment branch for coherent waveform recovery.
>
> We evaluate the system in three controlled mechanical-motion settings and 12 human-subject recordings across three indoor environments using a respiratory belt as reference. BreatheCS achieves a mean absolute respiratory-rate error of 0.405 breaths/min and a normalized waveform RMSE of 0.951, outperforming representative WiFi-derived PCA, MRC, and PCA–VMD baselines in joint rate-and-waveform performance. Ablation experiments show that tone diversity is the dominant and most consistent source of improvement, while the benefit of modal diversity is scene and quality dependent. These results establish measurement-aware fusion principles for physiological sensing with Bluetooth Channel Sounding.

## 对这个版本的说明

它刻意不写：

- Phase 无零陷；
- 顺序扫描导致连续相位；
- 等权三模态永远正确；
- BreatheCS 在每一个单项指标上都最好；
- 0.405 是全体方法中的绝对最低 BPM。

它强调的是：

> **joint rate-and-waveform performance**

这与你们的 Fig. 7 最一致：BreatheCS 的优势是同时输出较准 BPM 和较好波形，而不是单独 BPM 一项绝对最小。

# 六、Related Work 怎么组织？

Related Work 不建议按论文逐篇介绍。建议分成三个小节，并在每节最后指出“还缺什么”。

## 2.1 Contactless Respiration Sensing

这一节覆盖：

- FMCW/UWB/radar；
- WiFi CSI；
- RSSI/窄带无线；
- BLE legacy sensing。

重点不是全面综述，而是建立坐标：

| 技术     | 优点                     | 与本文差别           |
| -------- | ------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Radar    | 高采样率、距离分辨率高   | 需要专用硬件         |
| WiFi CSI | 子载波多、采样率通常较高 | 测量机制不同         |
| BLE RSSI | 低功耗、普及             | 只有粗粒度强度       |
| BLE CS   | 双向、多频点、相位相关   | 新测量结构，研究不足 |

结尾 gap：

> Existing respiration systems are dominated by WiFi CSI and radar. Their signal models and fusion assumptions do not directly describe the bidirectional PCT observations exposed by BLE CS.

------

## 2.2 WiFi CSI Modeling and Multi-Channel Fusion

这一节建议按照三条技术路线写。

### A. Fresnel-zone and multipath modeling

介绍：

- 呼吸引起路径长度变化；
- 幅值和相位取决于多径工作点；
- 不同子载波或链路具有不同灵敏度。

### B. Amplitude–phase complementarity

FullBreathe 利用 CSI 幅值和相位的正交互补关系处理 blind spots；FarSense 则利用双天线 CSI ratio 缓解相位偏置，并联合其幅相信息扩大呼吸感知范围。([researchers.mq.edu.au](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/fullbreathe-full-human-respiration-detection-exploiting-complemen/?utm_source=openai))

但要指出：

> 这些方法中的 amplitude/phase 通常来自同一 WiFi CSI 或 antenna ratio，而 BLE CS 的 Remote、Local 和 composite Phase 由双向 PCT 交换构成，不能直接视为同一种模态结构。

此外，FullBreathe 本身也指出单独使用 amplitude 或 phase 均可能存在 blind spots，二者的价值在于互补，而不是 phase 永远无盲区。([www-public.imtbs-tsp.eu](https://www-public.imtbs-tsp.eu/~zhang_da/pub/FullBreathe.pdf?utm_source=openai))

### C. Subcarrier/link fusion and signal decomposition

介绍：

- PCA/SVD；
- MRC/BNR weighting；
- VMD/EMD；
- candidate selection；
- antenna/link ratio。

Position-Free Breath Detection 利用 CSI ratio 的幅相信息和 PCA–VMD 融合来缓解位置与噪声问题，是合适的迁移 baseline。([researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/373624223_Position-Free_Breath_Detection_During_Sleep_via_Commodity_WiFi?utm_source=openai))

然后指出你们的区别：

> Prior approaches generally fuse channels to produce one signal and then derive respiration rate from that signal. BreatheCS instead separates spectral rate estimation from coherent waveform reconstruction because the two outputs have different sensitivity to temporal phase errors.

这是 Related Work 中最重要的 novelty comparison。

------

## 2.3 Bluetooth Sensing and Channel Sounding

这一节先说明：

- 传统 BLE sensing 多用 RSSI、AoA/AoD 或专有 channel measurements；
- Bluetooth Core 6.0 正式引入 CS；
- CS 原本用于精细、安全测距，包括 PBR 和 RTT。([bluetooth.com](https://www.bluetooth.com/core-specification-6-feature-overview/?utm_source=openai))
- 已有工作开始探索 BLE 6.0 CS 的 device-free sensing 能力，但仍主要停留在可行性展示。([doi.org](https://doi.org/10.1145/3714394.3754369?utm_source=openai))

然后明确本文和你们之前工作的关系：

> Our prior work demonstrated the feasibility of device-free sensing using BLE CS. This paper goes beyond feasibility by establishing a respiration-specific observation model, characterizing tone and modal relationships, designing separate rate and waveform processing branches, and evaluating the resulting system against physiological ground truth.

如果那篇 UbiComp Companion 论文就是你们团队的工作，必须主动引用，不能装作不存在。主动说明“feasibility paper → systematic full paper”，通常比让审稿人指出重叠安全得多。

------

## Related Work 的结束段

建议用一段统一收束：

> In summary, prior wireless respiration research has established Fresnel-based sensing models, amplitude–phase complementarity, and multi-channel fusion for WiFi CSI. Bluetooth CS introduces a different measurement primitive: bidirectional endpoint observations, a composite phase, sparse event sampling, and frequency-dependent tone relationships. Existing works have not systematically studied how these properties affect respiratory-rate estimation and waveform recovery, nor whether the two objectives should share the same fusion strategy. BreatheCS addresses this gap.

# 七、Conclusion 应该怎么写？

Conclusion 不应重复摘要所有技术细节，也不要再引入新的 Phase 零陷假说。

建议包含四部分：

1. 做了什么；
2. 得到了什么核心认识；
3. 结果；
4. 限制和未来方向。

## 推荐英文版本

> **Conclusion—** This work presented BreatheCS, a measurement-aware framework for contactless respiration sensing with Bluetooth Channel Sounding. By modeling the bidirectional PCT measurements, we showed that BLE CS provides two endpoint amplitude observations and one composite-phase observation with distinct complex-plane sensitivities and noise characteristics. Our measurements further revealed heterogeneous sensing quality across tones, continuous and scene-dependent phase relationships, and different processing requirements for respiratory-rate estimation and waveform recovery.
>
> Based on these findings, BreatheCS combines a shared quality-aware tone-fusion front-end with a spectral branch for robust BPM estimation and a hierarchical complex-domain branch for waveform reconstruction. Evaluation on controlled mechanical motion and 12 human-subject recordings achieved a mean BPM error of 0.405 breaths/min and a normalized waveform RMSE of 0.951. More importantly, the ablation results showed that tone diversity is the most reliable source of improvement, whereas modal diversity—particularly composite phase—provides conditional rather than universal benefit.
>
> These findings suggest that future BLE CS sensing systems should avoid directly migrating fixed WiFi CSI fusion assumptions and instead account for the bidirectional measurement structure and task-specific sensing objective. Future work will investigate observable conditions under which composite phase provides complementary information, improve robustness to body motion and complex multipath, and extend the framework to irregular breathing and apnea detection.

## 中文逻辑

Conclusion 中最值得留下的不是数字，而是三条 takeaway：

- BLE CS 不能被当成低采样率 WiFi CSI；
- tone diversity 是最可靠的增益；
- BPM 和波形应使用不同处理域，模态融合需要条件化。

------

# 八、Phase 独特角色应不应该放入本篇核心贡献？

我的建议是：

## 当前阶段：不要放入 Abstract 和主要贡献

原因是：

- “Phase 无零陷”模型需要修正；
- Phase rescue 尚未完成验证；
- HKH 上 Phase-only 明显较差；
- 当前门控指标还不能可靠识别 Phase 有效窗口；
- Phase 在 CS 与 HKH 两域的行为不一致。

目前最多在 Introduction insight 或 Discussion 中写：

> Our evaluation also reveals a pronounced domain-dependent behavior of the composite-phase observation, motivating conditional rather than unconditional modal fusion.

## 如果下一轮实验成功

如果满足以下条件：

1. Phase 增加了显著的 R/L/P oracle 上限；
2. Phase 独特救援窗口不只集中在一个 subject；
3. IQ 径向/切向分析支持互补投影解释；
4. 无 GT gate 可以识别这些窗口；
5. leave-one-subject-out 下仍有增益；

那么可以把 C3 或 C2 升级成：

> We identify composite phase as a conditional rescue modality and develop an observable confidence gate that activates it only when endpoint amplitude observations become unreliable.

否则就把 Phase 的跨域失败作为重要实验发现，不要强行把它包装为成功算法。

# 九、论文目前最大的潜在审稿风险

## 1. 自己的消融优于完整方法

Remote-only 0.376、Channel-only 0.381，而 BreatheCS 是 0.405。审稿人很可能问：

> Why is the proposed full method worse than its ablated variants?

建议回答方式不是隐藏，而是重新定义目标：

- BreatheCS 是**联合 BPM + waveform 系统**；
- Remote-only 没有多模态稳健性论证；
- Channel-only 不输出完整波形；
- BreatheCS 在 Fig. 7 上取得最佳 joint operating point；
- 但必须承认固定三模态融合并非所有场景都提升 BPM。

如果统计检验表明 0.376 和 0.405 没有显著差异，可以写：

> The full pipeline maintains comparable rate accuracy while providing the best joint rate-and-waveform performance.

如果显著更差，就应修改 BPM 模态策略，而不是回避。

## 2. “equal weight is physically correct”与实验冲突

Remote/Local 物理对等不代表 Remote、Local、Phase 三者应等权。

更不能从“没有先验知道谁更好”推出“等权最优”。正确表述应是：

> Equal weighting is a bias-free baseline, but not necessarily the statistically optimal fusion rule.

## 3. 只报告均值，不报告统计可靠性

0.405 与 0.435 的差距很小。最终论文至少需要：

- 12 recording 的 paired results；
- 95% CI；
- paired bootstrap 或 Wilcoxon；
- 按 subject/room 的稳定性；
- 不把重叠窗口当作独立样本。

# 十、最终推荐的论文定位和标题

## 最稳妥的定位

> **A measurement study and task-oriented sensing system for BLE Channel Sounding respiration monitoring.**

不是单纯的算法优化论文，也不是单纯的 Phase 理论论文。

## 推荐标题

### 方案 1：最稳妥

**BreatheCS: Measurement-Aware Respiratory Rate and Waveform Sensing with Bluetooth Channel Sounding**

### 方案 2：强调建模

**Breathing Sensing with Bluetooth Channel Sounding: Measurement Modeling and Task-Oriented Fusion**

### 方案 3：强调双向结构

**BreatheCS: Modeling and Fusing Bidirectional Bluetooth Channel Sounding Measurements for Respiration Sensing**

我最推荐第一个，因为：

- 有系统名；
- 突出 measurement-aware；
- 明确同时输出 rate 和 waveform；
- 不承诺尚未证明的 Phase rescue 或 sequential-sampling mechanism。

# 总结

你们现在的三个贡献**方向正确，但需要重新落点**：

- **C1 从“三个绝对物理约束”改为 BLE CS 呼吸观测模型与实证刻画；**
- **C2 从“统一解决所有约束”改为 BPM 与波形任务分解的双分支管线；**
- **C3 从“做了两组实验”升级为对 tone diversity、modal diversity 和 task-specific fusion 的系统量化与设计结论。**

整篇论文最稳妥的核心贡献不是某个具体的 $\eta\rho$ 权重，也不是尚未验证的 Phase 零陷救援，而是：

> **揭示 BLE CS 的双向观测结构如何改变呼吸感知中的信道融合与模态融合问题，并证明呼吸率和呼吸波形应采用不同的处理域和融合原则。**

这条主线与你们目前绝大多数模型、图、实验和结果都兼容，也能容纳下一轮 Phase 研究：Phase gate 成功，可以作为增强；失败，也不会推翻整篇论文。
